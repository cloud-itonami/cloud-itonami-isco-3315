# Business Model: Independent Valuation & Loss Assessment Practice

## Classification

- Repository: `cloud-itonami-isco-3315`
- ISCO-08: `3315`
- Occupation: Valuers and Loss Assessors
- Social impact: consumer-protection, local-jobs

## Customer

- insurance companies
- individuals
- property owners

## Offer

- property valuation
- loss assessment
- claim documentation

## Revenue

- per-assessment fee
- contract retainer

## Trust Controls

- no valuation issuance above the client's registered assessment-authority ceiling without governor gate
- site inspection required before any assessment is finalized
- valuation and assessment records are auditable, not editable
